Nothering is a personal network proxy that runs on your iPhone, with a free companion app for your Mac.

Start the proxy on your iPhone, connect your Mac, and the Nothering app for Mac routes your Mac's connections through the proxy. Apps on your Mac work as usual, with no per-app proxy settings.

HOW IT WORKS
- Tap Start Proxy on your iPhone.
- Connect your Mac with a USB cable, or over the iPhone's Personal Hotspot.
- Turn on Nothering in the Mac menu bar.

FEATURES
- Works over USB or Personal Hotspot, and picks the best link automatically.
- Supports TCP, UDP and DNS.
- Live upload and download speed, connection counts and data totals.
- A Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island shows when the proxy is running.
- Fails safe: when your iPhone is disconnected, your Mac goes back to its normal network.

PRIVACY
Nothering has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own. Nothing is collected or sent to the developer.

REQUIREMENTS
- The Nothering app for Mac (macOS 15 or later), available from the support site.
- Data used by your Mac counts toward your iPhone's data plan.

Nothering is open source under the MIT license.
