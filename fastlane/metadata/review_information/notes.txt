Nothering is a personal SOCKS5 proxy that runs on the iPhone (port 11080). A free, open source companion app for Mac (https://github.com/devxoul/nothering/releases) connects to it over a USB cable or Personal Hotspot and routes the Mac's connections through it. The proxy only serves the user's own Mac: it accepts connections from the USB link (loopback) and the Personal Hotspot interface, never from Wi-Fi or cellular peers.

How to test:
1. On the iPhone, open Nothering and tap Start Proxy. The status changes to Running.
2. Install the Mac app from the link above, connect the Mac to the iPhone with a USB cable, and turn on Nothering from the menu bar icon.
3. Load a website on the Mac. The iPhone app shows "Mac connected via USB" and the throughput chart updates.

Without a Mac, you can check the iPhone side only: after tapping Start Proxy the app shows Running and "Waiting for Mac...".

Background audio: Nothering is used by people working away from their usual network, such as in a cafe, a client's office or on a job site, where the surroundings are often noisy. The app has a Focus sound setting (White noise, Pink noise or Brown noise) that plays ambient noise to help them focus while they work on the Mac. It keeps playing while the iPhone is locked or the app is in the background, which is why the app uses the audio background mode. To hear it: choose a Focus sound under the status card, tap Start Proxy, then go to the Home Screen. Tapping Stop Proxy stops the sound immediately.

The app has no accounts and collects no data.
