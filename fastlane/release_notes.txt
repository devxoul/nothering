New Focus sound setting: pick White, Pink or Brown noise under the status card to hear ambient noise while the proxy is on.
Start the proxy with a Focus sound selected, go to the Home Screen and lock the iPhone, and check that the sound keeps playing and the Mac stays connected. Switch sounds while it plays, then tap Stop Proxy and check that the sound stops.
The "Hotspot data saved" headline now shows the current session while the proxy is on, and the last session after it stops.
