# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

Nothering doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.** The apps don't include any third-party SDKs that collect data.
- **No servers.** Nothering has no backend. Traffic from your Mac goes through your iPhone straight to the sites and services you use, the same way it would from the iPhone itself.
- **Nothing leaves your devices.** The iPhone app counts connections and bytes to show live statistics on screen. These numbers stay on the device and reset when the proxy stops.
- **Local diagnostics.** When a connection or DNS lookup fails, the apps write the destination host name, port and error to the system log on that device, to help troubleshoot. These entries stay in Apple's on-device log (viewable in Console) and are never sent to the developer.
- **Local network.** The iPhone app asks for local network access only to accept connections from your Mac over USB or Personal Hotspot. It refuses connections from any other network.
- **Updates.** The Mac app checks GitHub for new versions. GitHub may log the request like any other web request; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
